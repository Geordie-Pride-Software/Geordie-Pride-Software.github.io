Our story

Built from family roots and a practical ambition.

Geordie Pride Software was established in early 2025 by a small group of friends who wanted to create useful, dependable technology. Our work spans software, and games, with every project shaped by the same belief: good tools should work well and earn people’s trust.

The name comes from The Geordie Pride Lodge, a family-run business in West Yorkshire that has served its community since 1989. We carry that family name forward as a mark of heritage, care, and dependable work.
