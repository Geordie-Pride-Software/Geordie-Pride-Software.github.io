Geordie Pride Software

At Geordie Pride Software, we have what may be considered unusual roots. Originally established in early 2025 by myself and a few friends, we have been creating software, hardware, and games designed to be efficient, reliable, and built to work well. The name of our company originates from a business in West Yorkshire, owned and run by my family since 1989. This business is called: The Geordie Pride Lodge. In honour of my heritage and family history, our group decided to adopt the family name and create a company that produces software as dependable and trustworthy as the business my family has built over the years.
